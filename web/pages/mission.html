THE NAME / SEVEN WORDS

Seven words. One working direction.

Each word points to the work. “Of” simply connects the others.

Coalition Of Developers Advancing Responsible Intelligent Systems.

  1. C

    Coalition

    Developers working together around a shared purpose.

  2. O

    Of

    A link between the coalition and its members.

  3. D

    Developers

    The people who turn ideas into working systems.

  4. A

    Advancing

    Move knowledge and useful technology forward.

  5. R

    Responsible

    Take safety, security and consequences seriously.

  6. I

    Intelligent

    Systems that learn, reason or act with growing capability.

  7. S

    Systems

    Software, models and infrastructure working together.
