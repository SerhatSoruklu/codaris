MAKE THE CONVERSATION PRACTICAL

What would you like to contribute?

Share only information that is appropriate for an initial conversation. Do not send confidential or sensitive material through a general inquiry.

01

Name the technical area

For example: evaluation design, security review, implementation, data quality, interoperability or infrastructure.

02

Describe capacity and constraints

Explain the expertise, time, systems or resources available, along with relevant limits or dependencies.

03

Identify relevant affiliations

State the organization or funding relationship connected to the proposed contribution where relevant.

04

Set an expected outcome

Say what could be inspected or learned, and what evidence would count as progress.
