02 / CONTRIBUTION WITHOUT CONTROL

Support the work.
Do not buy its conclusions.

The organizational framework is in development. Participation, defined contributions and sponsorship or other support are separate relationships and should be disclosed separately. Funding will not purchase governance, technical control or working-group outcomes.
