Organization representatives.

People do the technical work. Their CODARIS contribution and their employer’s role should be clear, separate facts.

A future public record may identify representatives, their stated organizational affiliation, the work they contribute to and their published contribution history. No representatives directory is currently operating.

ROLE CLARITY

An employer title does not grant authority in CODARIS.

PROPOSED RECORD

Context around the contribution.

Publication and disclosure practices remain to be designed. Any future record should be accurate, relevant and handled with care.

01

State the person’s role

Describe what the individual contributes to a working group or initiative; do not substitute an employer job title for a CODARIS role.

02

Show affiliation separately

Record an organizational affiliation as context, not as evidence that the organization directs or owns the work.

03

Connect claims to work

Where a contribution history is published, link it to identifiable outputs such as reviews, code, evaluations or research.

04

Surface relevant interests

Define how employment, funding and other relevant interests are disclosed alongside the technical work they may relate to.
