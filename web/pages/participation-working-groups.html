PROPOSED OPERATING MODEL

Give the work a clear frame.

Specific charters make it easier to understand what a group is doing, how conclusions are reached and where more review is needed.

01

State the question and scope

Define the technical problem, intended users, boundaries and conditions that would change the group’s conclusions.

02

Make participation legible

Describe how contributors join, what expertise is useful, how conflicts are surfaced and who maintains the record.

03

Work from evidence

Use documented methods, reproducible tests and explicit assumptions where the subject allows.

04

Publish useful outputs

Possible results include threat models, specifications, reference implementations, test suites, interoperability reports and deployment guidance.

05

Keep review independent

Seek scrutiny beyond the contributing organization where feasible, and record unresolved disagreement and limitations.

06

Separate affiliation from authority

Employer affiliation is context. It does not grant ownership of the group or automatic decision rights.
