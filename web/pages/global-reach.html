HOW TO READ THE SIGNAL

A clearer picture of who is here.

These live totals describe the verified coalition as it grows. They are intentionally simple, transparent, and privacy-conscious.

01 / VERIFIED

People confirm their email

The member total includes accounts with a verified email address. New applications are counted after confirmation.

02 / SELF-REPORTED

Members choose their country

Country totals come from the country members provide in their profile. CODARIS does not infer location from an IP address.

03 / AGGREGATE

Community counts stay public

We show totals, not a public member directory. Names, email addresses, and application responses are not part of these figures. Read the privacy notice.
