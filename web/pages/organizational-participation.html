CODARIS / ORGANIZATIONAL PARTICIPATION

◇ Coming soon

Build with CODARIS.

A proposed framework for organizations to contribute engineering, research, infrastructure and support to shared technical work, with safeguards intended to keep contributions separate from control over outcomes.

CODARIS is developing this framework for engineering organizations, research groups, foundations, standards communities and open-source projects. Participation will be documented, contribution-led and independent of any single institution.

THE GOVERNING DISTINCTION

Participation is based on documented contributions and defined roles; sponsorship alone would not confer decision-making authority.

FUNDING ≠ GOVERNANCE SUPPORT ≠ TECHNICAL CONTROL EMPLOYER ≠ CODARIS AUTHORITY

PROPOSED SAFEGUARDS / IN DEVELOPMENT

Protect independent technical judgment.

These safeguards are proposals for the participation framework. They are not yet adopted procedures or current program guarantees.

01

Support brings no control

When adopted, sponsorship would not grant reserved seats, vetoes, guaranteed adoption of proposals or preferential access to decisions.

02

Disclose relevant interests

Participants would disclose relevant employment, funding and other interests, with disclosures recorded alongside the work they relate to.

03

Review against evidence

Technical proposals would be assessed against published criteria and evidence, with review beyond the contributing organization sought where feasible.

04

Monitor concentrated influence

CODARIS would track organizational representation in working groups and decision roles. Where participation becomes concentrated, it would disclose the imbalance and consider broader review or limits on concentrated decision authority.

05

Make concerns reportable

A defined route would let participants raise conflicts of interest or concerns about whether the process is being followed.

WHY PARTICIPATE

Work on problems that cross boundaries.

Participation is about producing and reviewing technical work. It does not promise commercial advantage, endorsement or preferential access.

01

Shared engineering problems

Work with independent developers on challenges that span organizational boundaries.

02

Interoperability

Test mechanisms across independent implementations rather than a single stack.

03

Early technical input

Contribute evidence and implementation experience while specifications are being developed.

04

Open technical output

Help create specifications, reference implementations, tests and deployment guidance others can inspect.

05

Independent review

Expose designs to engineers outside an organization’s internal development environment.

06

Documented contribution

Where appropriate, CODARIS intends to record organizational and individual contributions transparently.

02 / ORGANIZATION REPRESENTATIVES

Participation happens through people.

◇ Coming soon

A future representatives directory may record a person’s name, stated role and organization, relevant working groups, contribution areas and public contribution history.

Organization affiliation and CODARIS standing will be shown separately. An employer does not automatically grant a person authority within CODARIS; roles will depend on documented contributions and CODARIS processes.

03 / SPONSORED INITIATIVES

Disclose material support around the work.

◇ Coming soon

Where initiatives receive material support, a future record will describe the initiative, supporter, type and period of support, relevant disclosures, and how technical decisions remain independent.

Potential support includes direct sponsorship, research grants, infrastructure credits, compute, event support, tooling and other in-kind resources. No sponsorship accounting or active sponsorship program is represented here.

04 / SUPPORTERS & FUNDING

Separate financial support from governance.

◇ Coming soon

CODARIS intends to disclose grants, sponsorship, infrastructure credits, research funding, event support and in-kind contributions in a clear, reviewable record.

The proposed framework is intended to prevent funding from determining technical conclusions or buying governance, technical control, standards influence or preferential treatment. CODARIS plans to distinguish supporter, participant, collaborator, sponsor, grant funder, infrastructure supporter and vendor; these labels are not interchangeable.

CODARIS-WIDE TECHNICAL WORK

Working groups remain independent of employers.

◇ Coming soon

CODARIS intends for working groups to be initiatives where individuals can contribute to a defined technical remit. An organization’s representative may contribute to a future group, but the group remains a CODARIS effort and its outputs are assessed through documented process, evidence and review.

Working-group participation, organization affiliation and financial support are separate facts. No organization owns a working group by participating in or supporting its work.
