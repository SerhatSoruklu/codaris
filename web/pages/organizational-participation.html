CODARIS / ORGANIZATIONAL PARTICIPATION

◇ Framework in development

Build with CODARIS.

A contribution framework for organizations that want to advance responsible intelligent systems through inspectable technical work.

CODARIS is shaping clear routes for engineering, research, infrastructure and financial support. The aim is collaboration with documented contributions and independent technical judgment.
