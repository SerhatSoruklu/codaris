CODARIS / PRIVACY

Your data at CODARIS.

Last updated: 24 September 2026.

Who is responsible

CODARIS is operated by Serhat Soruklu, 3 Culross Close, London, N15 3RH, United Kingdom. For privacy questions and requests, email privacy@codaris.org.

What we collect and why

When you create an account, we collect your name, email, country, role, reason for joining and any optional profile picture, LinkedIn, GitHub or website links you provide. Uploaded pictures are reduced to 100 × 100 pixels; original files and metadata are not stored. We store a password hash, your membership ID, email verification state, session records and learning progress. We use this information to provide the account and membership services you request. Our lawful basis for these core services is performance of our agreement with you, including steps you request before entering it.

We process limited request and security information to prevent abuse, investigate faults and protect accounts. We rely on our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure. Please do not include sensitive personal information in your joining reason or profile links.

Verification and visibility

Email verification confirms access to a mailbox. It does not verify your identity, country, role or ownership of external profiles. Changing your email immediately clears email verification until you confirm the new address. Your original joining reason is retained as your application record and cannot be edited through settings; contact us if you need a correction or erasure.

Your account settings are not a public directory. Public community statistics contain aggregate counts of verified members and self-reported countries. We do not publish your name, email or application response in those statistics.

Cookies

We use a necessary session cookie, codaris_session, to keep you signed in for up to 12 hours. It is not used for advertising. The application does not place advertising or analytics cookies.

Service providers

Infrastructure and email providers process information needed to host the service, maintain its database and deliver account emails. Our outbound account emails use Google Workspace, with CODARIS as the sender name and admin@coupyn.com as the sending mailbox. Support mail is routed to the operator’s administrative mailbox. External websites linked from CODARIS have their own privacy practices.

Providers may process information outside the UK. Before enabling such processing, we assess the transfer arrangements and put the required safeguards in place. Contact us for information about the safeguards applicable to your data.

Retention

Account details, your application response and learning progress are retained while your account remains open. You can request account deletion by contacting us. We may retain limited information where necessary to meet a legal obligation or resolve a dispute, and will explain the reason where applicable. Verification links expire after 24 hours; password reset links expire after 30 minutes. Expired sessions and tokens are removed by scheduled maintenance. Delivery records are removed after 30 days, and request-limit records after one day. Deleted data may remain in protected backups until those backups rotate out of retention.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction or deletion of personal data, restriction of processing, and a portable copy where applicable. These rights depend on the circumstances and lawful basis. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

You have the right to object to processing based on our legitimate interests. Contact privacy@codaris.org to exercise that right.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. We do not use your account data for advertising profiles or automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.
