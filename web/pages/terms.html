These terms govern your use of the CODARIS website, account and membership features. The operator’s legal status and full contact details should be confirmed before public account registration is enabled.

By submitting the registration form, you agree to these terms and acknowledge the Privacy notice. If you do not agree, do not submit the form. The current release has no payment or subscription feature.

02

Applications and membership

To apply, provide the required registration information, a working email address and a joining statement. Optional profile links and a profile image are not required. You must provide information that is accurate to the best of your knowledge and update the profile fields that account settings allow you to change.

Membership becomes active when you confirm access to your email address. The implemented service does not include a staff application-review or approval step. Email confirmation verifies mailbox access; it does not verify your identity, professional credentials, country or ownership of external profiles.

Your membership credential has a separate public verification link. By selecting the required registration acknowledgement, you understand that anyone with that link or its QR code can see your display name, role, public membership number, current membership status and issue date. CODARIS has no public member directory. You can turn off public verification in your dashboard; verification then returns a generic unavailable response.

Your randomly generated membership ID identifies your account and can be used to sign in. It is not a password, qualification, accreditation or endorsement by CODARIS. No age requirement is implemented in the registration flow; CODARIS’s age-eligibility policy has not yet been set.

03

Accounts and security

Choose a unique password and keep it confidential. Do not share your password or verification and recovery links. Tell us promptly at contact@codaris.org if you believe someone else has accessed your account.

You can change your password and email address through account settings. A new email address must be verified before member access resumes. Password reset and password change sign out existing sessions. The service may limit requests to protect accounts and the service.

04

How members should act

Use CODARIS lawfully and in a way that respects other people and the service. In particular, do not:

  • impersonate another person or misrepresent your qualifications, affiliations or control of a profile;
  • attempt to access another account, bypass security controls or disrupt the site;
  • submit information or links that you do not have the right to provide, or that infringe another person’s rights; or
  • use the service to break the law or harm others.

These rules apply to information you submit, including your joining statement, profile image and external links. CODARIS does not provide a public member directory, chat or member-posted content. Public credential verification is limited to the fields described above.

Your original joining statement is stored as part of the account record and cannot be changed in settings. Contact privacy@codaris.org to request a correction or account closure. How closure and erasure requests are handled is explained in the Privacy notice.
