◇ Planned

Sponsored initiatives.

Make material support visible around the work it helps make possible.

CODARIS has no active sponsorship program or sponsorship records. If initiative sponsorship is introduced, its purpose, scope and material support should be described plainly, with technical responsibility kept independent.

How funding is treated

SUPPORT THE CONDITIONS

Funding can enable work. It should not predetermine what the work finds.

DISCLOSE THE SUPPORTREVIEW THE OUTPUT

A TRANSPARENT INITIATIVE RECORD

Report what matters.

A future record could help readers understand the connection between an initiative and its material support.

01

Define the work

Identify the initiative, its technical scope and the outputs it intends to produce.

02

Describe material support

State the supporter, form and relevant period of funding or in-kind contribution.

03

Disclose decision boundaries

Explain who is responsible for technical review and how support is kept separate from findings and conclusions.

04

Publish results with limits

Where work is released, include methods, evidence and limitations so others can inspect and challenge it.
