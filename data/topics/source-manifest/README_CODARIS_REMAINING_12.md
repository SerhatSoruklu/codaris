## Research rules applied

- Keep taxonomic layers explicit rather than calling unlike tools the same thing.
- Preserve source-specific popularity/adoption metrics and their denominators.
- Do not average incomparable metrics into a universal ranking.
- Do not infer per-tool country or gender data from overall survey demographics.
- Retain some legacy technologies for educational/history coverage without implying current recommendation.
- Include source provenance directly in CSV/JSON/XLSX files.
