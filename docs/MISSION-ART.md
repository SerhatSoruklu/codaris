This is a serious team building and verifying intelligent systems, quiet concentration before a mission.
Composition: wide architectural shot at seated eye height, 28mm cinema lens. People and detailed computer stations concentrated in the central and right two-thirds; the left third is quieter dark architectural negative space for website headline overlay. Frame includes ceiling architecture, dramatic depth, a long briefing table and subtle reflections. Scene must feel rich and inhabitable, not generic sci-fi illustration.
Lighting: motivated cyan screen light, narrow white overhead strip lights, subtle atmospheric haze catching light, physically plausible bounce lighting and ray-traced reflections, deep navy shadows with visible detail. Palette near-black #080e16, cyan #59e4eb, steel grey, natural skin tones. Restrained cinematic color grading, beautiful material realism, cloth stitching, brushed metal and smoked glass. Clear silhouettes, high detail, no excessive bloom.
Constraints: no weapons, no combat, no national flags, no skulls, no real military insignia, no hooded hacker cliches, no floating holograms, no giant text, no watermarks, no UI frame or website mockup.
